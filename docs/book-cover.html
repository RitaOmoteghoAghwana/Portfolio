Why it matters

Readers judge books by their covers.

01

Your cover is your first ad

In a sea of thumbnails on Amazon, your cover is a 2-second decision. A professional design signals quality and genre instantly — an amateur one loses the sale before the reader ever sees your blurb.

02

Genre expectations are real

Romance readers, thriller readers and business book readers all have visual expectations. A cover that fits the genre earns trust before the reader knows anything about you or your writing.

03

One design, every platform

eBook thumbnail, paperback wrap, audiobook art, social media graphics — one cohesive identity that works everywhere you publish, in every format your readers find you.

The process

Cover in hand in 5 steps.

01

Brief

Genre, tone, target reader, title, author name, any imagery ideas and cover references you love. The more I know, the better the first concepts.

02

Concepts

I create 2–3 distinct design directions and present them for your feedback. You choose the concept that resonates most.

03

Refine

Revisions on the chosen concept until every detail — type, colour, composition, imagery — is exactly right.

04

Finalise

Full print wrap sized to your page count, eBook and audiobook versions prepared and print specs checked for KDP / IngramSpark compliance.

05

Deliver

All files handed over — print PDF, eBook JPG/PNG, source file and the brand guide. Ready to upload and publish.

FAQ

Questions people always ask.

01

Do you need to read my manuscript to design the cover?

+

No — I don't need the full manuscript. A brief that covers your genre, the tone of the book, your target reader and any imagery ideas is enough to get started. A short blurb or synopsis is helpful too, but not required.

02

What file formats will I receive?

+

You'll receive a print-ready PDF (correct bleed and colour profile for KDP and IngramSpark), a high-resolution JPG and PNG for the eBook front, and an audiobook square if included in your package. The Author Brand package also includes the source file so you can make edits yourself in future.

03

Can you design a series of covers that match?

+

Yes — series consistency is something I specialise in. Once the first cover is designed, each additional book in the series receives a discount. Message me with the number of books in your series and I'll put together a custom quote.

04

What if I don't like any of the initial concepts?

+

If none of the initial concepts hit the mark, I'll ask more questions and create a new round at no extra charge. My goal is a cover you're proud of — the revision process exists to get us there together.
